# How to Play Belot

Belot (Белот) is the most popular card game in Bulgaria. It is a trick-taking game for four
players in two teams, a little like a fast, punchy cousin of Bridge. This guide assumes you
have never played before. You can read it top to bottom, or jump to the part you need.

The rules follow [belot.bg](https://belot.bg/en/rules-belot/), the reference this game is built on.

## 1. The goal

You and your partner play against the other two players. The game is a series of short
**deals** (rounds). In each deal one team commits to a **contract**, everyone plays out their
cards, and both teams earn points for the cards they capture and for any bonus
**combinations** they hold.

**The first team to reach 151 points wins the game.**

## 2. Players, teams and the deck

- **Four players, two teams.** Partners sit opposite each other. In this app you sit at the
  bottom (South), your partner sits at the top (North), and the two opponents sit left (West)
  and right (East).
- **Play goes counter-clockwise**, to the right. After you, East plays, then your partner,
  then West.
- **The deck has 32 cards:** 7, 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King and Ace in each of the four suits
  (clubs ♣, diamonds ♦, hearts ♥, spades ♠). It is a normal deck with the 2s to 6s removed.

## 3. How strong is each card, and what is it worth?

This is the part that surprises new players most. In Belot a card has **two different
properties**:

- its **strength**: which card wins when two of the same suit meet, and
- its **point value**: what it is worth when your team captures it.

Both depend on whether the suit is **trump** (the "boss" suit for this deal, chosen during
bidding) or not.

### Trump suit

| Card     | J  | 9  | A  | 10 | K | Q | 8 | 7 |
|----------|----|----|----|----|---|---|---|---|
| Strength | 1st (strongest) | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th | 6th | 7th | 8th (weakest) |
| Points   | 20 | 14 | 11 | 10 | 4 | 3 | 0 | 0 |

The Jack and the 9, normally weak cards, become the two most powerful cards in the game when
their suit is trump.

### Non-trump suits

| Card     | A  | 10 | K | Q | J | 9 | 8 | 7 |
|----------|----|----|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strength | 1st (strongest) | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th | 6th | 7th | 8th (weakest) |
| Points   | 11 | 10 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Note that the **10 is stronger than the King** in every suit.

### Which table applies?

That depends on the contract (see [Bidding](#5-bidding-choosing-the-contract)):

| Contract | Bulgarian | Which suits use the trump table |
|----------|-----------|---------------------------------|
| A suit (clubs, diamonds, hearts or spades) | Спатия, Каро, Купа, Пика | Only the chosen suit |
| **No Trumps** | Без коз | None. Every suit uses the non-trump table |
| **All Trumps** | Всичко коз | All four. Every suit uses the trump table |

A trump card beats any card of another suit, but only in a suit contract. In No Trumps and All
Trumps no suit beats another: only cards of the suit that was led can win the trick.

## 4. Dealing

1. The dealer shuffles and deals each player **3 cards, then 2 more**, so everyone holds
   **5 cards**.
2. Everyone looks at their 5 cards and **bids** (next section).
3. Once a contract is agreed, the dealer gives each player **3 more cards**, so everyone holds
   **8 cards** for play.

The role of dealer moves one seat counter-clockwise (to the right) after every deal.

## 5. Bidding: choosing the contract

Bidding decides **what is trump** and **which team has to prove it can win** the deal.

### How a bid works

- Bidding starts with the player to the **dealer's right** and goes counter-clockwise.
- On your turn you either **pass** (Пас) or announce a contract. A contract must be **higher**
  than the last one announced. From lowest to highest:

  **Clubs ♣ < Diamonds ♦ < Hearts ♥ < Spades ♠ < No Trumps < All Trumps**

- Bidding ends when a contract has been followed by **three passes in a row**. The last
  contract announced is the one played, and the team that announced it is the **declaring
  team**.
- If **all four players pass** without anyone bidding, the cards are collected and the next
  dealer deals again.

### Double and Redouble

- If the last contract was announced by an **opponent**, you may say **Double** (Контра)
  instead of a new contract. A double raises the stakes: see [scoring a doubled
  deal](#doubled-and-redoubled-deals).
- If your team's contract has been doubled, you or your partner may answer with **Redouble**
  (Реконтра), raising the stakes again.
- You cannot double your own partner's contract.
- A double does not end the bidding. Anyone may still announce a **higher contract**, which
  wipes out the double and starts a fresh contract.

### What should I bid? (beginner tips)

- **A suit contract** is a good choice when you hold the **Jack** and/or **9** of a suit, plus
  one or two more cards of it.
- **All Trumps** favours a hand with several Jacks and 9s.
- **No Trumps** favours a hand with several Aces and 10s.
- Remember you only see 5 of your 8 cards when you bid.

## 6. Playing the cards

A deal is played as **8 tricks**. In each trick every player plays one card, going
counter-clockwise.

- The player to the **dealer's right** leads the first trick and may play any card.
- The trick is won by the **strongest trump** in it, or if there is no trump, by the
  **strongest card of the suit that was led**. A card of another non-trump suit never wins.
- The winner of a trick collects the four cards and **leads the next trick**.

### What you must play

Belot is strict about which card you may play. In this app, cards you are not allowed to play
are disabled, so you will learn the rules as you go. Here they are in full.

**In every contract: follow suit.** If you have a card of the suit that was led, you must
play one.

**Suit contract (one suit is trump):**

1. **Follow suit** if you can.
2. **When trump is led, raise.** If you can beat the strongest trump on the table, you must
   do so, even if it is your partner's card.
3. **If you cannot follow suit:**
   - If your **partner** is currently winning the trick, you may play **any card**. You do not
     have to trump.
   - If an **opponent** is currently winning the trick and you hold a trump, you **must trump**.
   - If an opponent has already trumped and is winning, you must **overtrump** (play a higher
     trump) if you can. If you cannot beat their trump, you may play any card.

**No Trumps:** just follow suit. If you cannot, play anything.

**All Trumps:**

1. **Follow suit** if you can.
2. **Raise:** if you can beat the strongest card of the led suit on the table, you must,
   **no matter who is winning**, even if it is your partner.
3. If you cannot follow suit, play anything.

### Example

Hearts are trump. West leads the K♠ and your partner plays the 7♠. You have no spades.
An opponent (West) is winning, so if you hold a heart you **must** play it. If instead your
partner had played the A♠, your partner would be winning and you could play any card.

## 7. Combinations (bonus points)

Combinations are special groups of cards in your hand that earn extra points. They are
called **announcements**, because you announce them during play. There are **no
combinations in No Trumps**.

### Sequences

Three or more cards **in a row in the same suit**. For sequences the cards are always in
their natural order, whatever the contract: 7, 8, 9, 10, J, Q, K, A.

| Name | Bulgarian | Cards | Points |
|------|-----------|-------|--------|
| Tierce | Терца | 3 in a row (e.g. 8♥ 9♥ 10♥) | 20 |
| Quarte | Кварта | 4 in a row | 50 |
| Quint  | Квинта | 5 in a row | 100 |

### Four of a kind ("square", Каре)

| Four of a kind | Points |
|----------------|--------|
| Four Jacks | 200 |
| Four 9s | 150 |
| Four Aces, 10s, Kings or Queens | 100 |
| Four 8s or 7s | nothing |

A run longer than five cards still counts as one quint (the top five cards). All eight cards
of a suit count as a quint plus a tierce (120).

A card can only be used once. If a card could belong to both four of a kind and a sequence,
the player chooses which one it counts for. The app picks whichever split is worth more.

### When combinations are announced

Sequences and four of a kind are announced in the **first trick**, as you play your first
card. The app does this for you automatically.

### Comparing sequences: only one team scores them

If both teams have sequences, they are compared and **only the team with the best sequence
scores**, but then it scores **all** of its sequences.

1. The longer sequence wins (a quint beats a quarte beats a tierce).
2. If they are the same length, the one with the higher top card wins (A-K-Q beats K-Q-J).
3. If the best sequences are exactly equal, **neither team** scores any sequences.

Four of a kind is compared separately: if both teams have one, only the higher-valued one
counts. Having four of a kind does not cancel the other team's sequences, and vice versa.

### Belot (Белот)

The **King and Queen of the trump suit** in the same hand form a **Belot**, worth **20
points**.

- It is announced when you **play the first of the two cards**, not at the start.
- In a suit contract it only counts in the **trump suit**.
- In **All Trumps** any suit counts, but only if you play the K or Q while **leading** that
  suit or **following suit** to it.
- There is **no Belot in No Trumps**.
- Belot is never cancelled by the other team's combinations. The K and Q can also be part of
  a sequence at the same time.

## 8. Scoring a deal

### Step 1: add up raw points

Each team adds:

- the point values of all cards in the tricks it won,
- **10 points** for winning the **last trick** ("last ten"),
- its combinations that counted, and its Belot.

Without combinations, the card points in a deal always add up to the same total:

| Contract | Total raw points |
|----------|------------------|
| Suit | 162 |
| All Trumps | 258 |
| No Trumps | 130, **doubled to 260** |

In **No Trumps** all card points are **doubled** (this is what makes it a high-stakes bid).

### Step 2: did the declaring team make it?

Compare the two teams' raw points:

- **Made it ("outside").** The declaring team has **more** points than the opponents. Each team
  scores its own points.
- **Failed ("inside", вътре).** The declaring team has **fewer** points. The opponents score
  **everything**, both their own points and the declarers'.
- **Hanging (висящи).** Both teams have exactly the same points. See
  [Special situations](#hanging-points).

### Step 3: divide by 10 and round

Raw points are divided by 10 and rounded to get game points. For example, 92 raw points
become 9 game points. A suit deal without combinations is worth 16 game points in total, and
All Trumps and No Trumps are worth 26.

Rounding is done so the two teams' game points add up to the right total. The team with fewer
points is rounded first, and the other team gets the rest:

- **Suit contract:** a score ending in **6 or more** rounds up, **5 or less** rounds down.
  Example: 86 vs 76 gives 8 and 8.
- **All Trumps:** a score ending in **4 or more** rounds up. Example: 134 vs 124 gives 13 and 13.
- **No Trumps:** each team rounds normally after doubling.

### Example

Spades are trump. Your team declared and won 102 raw points; the opponents won 60.
102 is more than 60, so you made it. You score 10 and the opponents score 6.

If you had won only 70 against their 92, you would be "inside", and the opponents would
score all 16.

## 9. Special situations

### Valat (all the tricks, Валат)

If one team wins **all 8 tricks**, it scores a **bonus of 90 points** on top of everything
else. If the deal was doubled or redoubled, the valat bonus is doubled or quadrupled along with
everything else.

### Doubled and redoubled deals

- When a deal is **doubled**, the team with **more** raw points takes **all** the points of the
  deal, **times 2**.
- When it is **redoubled**, the same applies **times 4**.

It does not matter who doubled: the stronger team takes everything. Doubling is a gamble for
both sides.

### Hanging points

If the two teams finish a deal with **exactly equal** raw points:

- the opponents of the declaring team score their own half straight away;
- the declaring team's half is **left hanging** and goes to **whichever team wins the next
  deal**;
- hanging points keep adding up if it happens again.

If a **doubled** or **redoubled** deal ends equal, **all** of its points hang and go to the
winner of the next deal.

### Nobody bids

If all four players pass, nobody scores. The cards are reshuffled and the next dealer deals.

## 10. Winning the game

- The game ends when a team reaches **151 points or more** at the end of a deal.
- If both teams pass 151 in the same deal, the **higher total** wins. If the totals are equal,
  play continues until one team is ahead.
- Under belot.bg rules a game **cannot be won with a valat**: if the deal that takes a team
  past 151 was a valat, play continues until a deal without a valat decides it.

## Quick reference

**Card strength**

- Trump: **J 9 A 10 K Q 8 7**
- Non-trump: **A 10 K Q J 9 8 7**

**Card points**

| | J | 9 | A | 10 | K | Q | 8 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trump | 20 | 14 | 11 | 10 | 4 | 3 | 0 | 0 |
| Non-trump | 2 | 0 | 11 | 10 | 4 | 3 | 0 | 0 |

**Contracts, low to high:** ♣ < ♦ < ♥ < ♠ < No Trumps < All Trumps

**Bonuses**

| Bonus | Points |
|-------|--------|
| Last trick | 10 |
| Belot (K+Q of trump) | 20 |
| Tierce | 20 |
| Quarte | 50 |
| Quint | 100 |
| Four Jacks | 200 |
| Four 9s | 150 |
| Four A, 10, K or Q | 100 |
| Valat (all 8 tricks) | 90 |

**Must-play rules:** follow suit; raise when trump is led (and always in All Trumps); if you
can't follow and an opponent is winning, trump or overtrump if you can.

|------|-----------|---------|

| Trump | Коз | The suit that beats all others in a suit contract |
| Contract | Обява | What the declaring team commits to: a suit, No Trumps or All Trumps |
| Declaring team | | The team whose contract is being played |
| Pass | Пас | Decline to bid |
| Double / Redouble | Контра / Реконтра | Raise the stakes ×2 / ×4 |
| Belot | Белот | King and Queen of trump, 20 points |

| Valat | Валат | One team wins all 8 tricks |
| Inside | Вътре | The declaring team failed its contract |
| Hanging points | Висящи | Points from a tied deal, carried to the next winner |
| Last ten | Последни десет | 10 points for winning the last trick |
